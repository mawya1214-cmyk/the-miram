Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

THE MIRAM official website is a static informational website presenting the film, its creative team, production stills and press materials. We respect visitors' privacy and aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

Information We Collect

The website does not require account registration and does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information. If you contact the filmmaker by email, your email address and the information you choose to include will be used only to respond to your inquiry and manage the communication.

Third-Party Services

The website may display content hosted by third-party services, including Google Drive for the official trailer and Google Fonts for typography. These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies. The website does not control their data practices.

Cookies

This website does not intentionally use advertising or tracking cookies. A language preference may be stored locally in your browser to remember whether you selected English or Arabic. This preference remains on your device and is not submitted to the filmmaker.

External Links

Links to external websites or services are provided for convenience. When you follow an external link, the external service's own terms and privacy policy apply.
