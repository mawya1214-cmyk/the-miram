THE FILM

A Story of Light, Hope and Resilience

THE MIRAM is a Sudanese AI-assisted 3D animated short film about hope surviving in the shadow of war.

Through the journey of a young Sudanese girl, the film explores education, separation, memory and the enduring strength of hope.

Generative AI was used as a filmmaking tool across visual development, storytelling, character and environment design, cinematic production and digital post-production.

THE STORY

When hope becomes a journey

“THE LIGHT THAT NO WAR CAN DIM.”

Hawa is a 19-year-old Sudanese girl whose life is transformed by conflict. Forced away from home, she carries with her the dream of education and the memory of the people she loves.

Her journey eventually leads her to Al Dabbah, where she sits for the Sudanese Secondary Certificate examination.

Against extraordinary circumstances, Hawa achieves third place in Sudan. Then comes the discovery that changes everything: her mother is still alive.
