THE MIRAM is a 7-minute 15-second AI-assisted 3D animated short following Hawa, a 19-year-old student from Darfur. When war tears her world apart, she begins a dangerous journey across the desert. Hunger, thirst and grief test her — but her dream of education survives.

Muawia Ismail is a Sudanese filmmaker, screenwriter and AI animation creator dedicated to telling meaningful African stories through emerging digital filmmaking technologies.

Through THE MIRAM, he explores resilience, education, family and hope while presenting Sudanese identity and landscapes through cinematic 3D animation.
