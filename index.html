WRITER • PRODUCER • DIRECTOR

Muawia
Ismail Ibrahim

Muawia Ismail Ibrahim is a Sudanese filmmaker, writer, producer, and AI Prompt Engineering specialist with a strong interest in digital storytelling, emerging technologies, African heritage, and historical narratives.

His creative work explores the use of generative artificial intelligence as a filmmaking tool, integrating AI-assisted storytelling, visual development, character and environment design, cinematic production, and digital post-production.

Muawia is also a member of the Asia Digital Art and Design Association (ADADA). His professional interests focus on exploring new approaches to independent filmmaking and expanding opportunities for African creators through emerging digital technologies.

He is committed to developing innovative cinematic projects that connect cultural heritage and contemporary technology while maintaining a strong emphasis on storytelling, visual identity, and creative direction.
