A STORY OF LIGHT

In the shadow of war, a young Sudanese girl discovers that hope can survive even in the darkest places.

THE MIRAM is a Sudanese AI-assisted 3D animated short film about resilience, education, family, and the enduring power of hope.

The film explores how a young person's dream can remain alive despite displacement, uncertainty, and conflict.

THE STORY

THE LIGHT THAT REMAINS

SUDAN

Hawa's journey begins with a question.

Hawa is a 19-year-old Sudanese girl whose life is transformed by war and displacement.

Leaving Darfur and traveling toward Al Dabbah, she continues her education while carrying the weight of everything she has lost.

After sitting for the Sudanese Secondary Certificate examination, Hawa achieves an extraordinary result — ranking third in Sudan.

But the greatest surprise is still waiting for her: she discovers that her mother is alive in hospital.

THE DIRECTOR

MUAWIA ISMAIL IBRAHIM

SUDANESE FILMMAKER · WRITER · PRODUCER · AI PROMPT ENGINEERING

Muawia Ismail Ibrahim is a Sudanese filmmaker, writer, producer, and AI Prompt Engineering specialist working at the intersection of cinema, generative AI, digital storytelling, and emerging technologies.

His creative work explores AI-assisted storytelling, visual development, character and environment design, cinematic production, and digital post-production.

His professional affiliations include the International Artificial Intelligence Committee (IAIC), AI Cinematic Films Guild (AICFG), and Asia Digital Art and Design Association (ADADA).
